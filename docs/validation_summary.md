# Dataset validation summary (Units 1 to 3)

Final validated dataset: **1,861 games x 58 columns** (`data/processed/games_clean.parquet`), **1,648,387 reviews x 34 columns** (`data/processed/reviews_clean/`, 5 parquet parts), **29,918 patch and sale events** (`data/processed/events.parquet`). Collected by our own scrapers on 1 to 2 October 2026 from public Steam sources; no pre-built dataset is used. Full dictionary: `docs/data_documentation.md` section 5. Samples: `data/sample/`.

| Check | Finding | Correction / evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Missing values and duplicates | 4,612 reviews with empty text; no duplicate review IDs or game IDs; large gaps in some game columns (about 98% of games have no Metacritic score, about 62% no controller-support entry, about 22% no achievement count); free games have no price; 12 reviews with unknown playtime | The 4,612 empty reviews are dropped (1,652,999 to 1,648,387). Columns that are mostly empty are not used as features. Controller support and achievements default to "none listed". Free games are priced at 0. Unknown playtime is flagged, not imputed (`docs/cleaning_log.md`) |
| Types, ranges and consistency | Timestamps in mixed forms; playtime in minutes; playtime outliers (above the 99.5% quantile of 363 hours: 8,242 reviews); 318 games with a wrong launch date; 30,018 reviews written before launch; 208,724 very short reviews, 4,951 symbol-spam and 2,285 copy-pasted reviews | Timestamps converted to UTC; playtime winsorised in a copy and flagged; launch dates corrected; the rest flagged and kept so each analysis chooses its own rule. 32 rules, each logged with counts |
| Suitability across all units | Unit 1: all games and reviews. Unit 2: 1,573,436 reviews with features (five very large games capped and left out of the review tables) and 1,490 games with a success tier. Unit 3: 557,202 English reviews for text mining; 1,490 games for 137 transaction items in association rules; 1,685 patch comparisons from 29,918 events | One shared split by game (1,191 train, 299 test games; 1,257,095 train and 316,341 test reviews), 5 folds inside train. `python -m src.features.validate` runs 21 checks (no game on both sides, features fitted on train only, no outcome columns as features); all pass. `python -m src.analysis.validate_review2` runs 21 checks on the Review 2 outputs; all pass |
| Synthetic data | Not applicable | All records come from public Steam endpoints |

Coverage: 99.95% of the reviews Steam reports for the 1,856 games scraped in full; the five largest games (Megabonk, Cult of the Lamb, Hades II, Balatro, Vampire Survivors) are capped at their newest 15,000 reviews. Raw data is frozen with row counts and SHA256 hashes (`docs/data_manifest.md`); reviewer IDs are salted hashes and no names are stored.
